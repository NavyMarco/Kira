## 2. Description of Service

Kira is a Discord bot designed to provide server management and automated community utilities, including:
* **Leveling & XP Systems:** Level tracking, XP perks, role rewards, and command restrictions.
* **Staff Protocol & Tracking:** Role change logs, promotion/demotion alerts, message/mention analytics, and quarantine lockdowns.
* **Moderation Suite:** Timeout, kick, ban, unban, warning tracking, and clear warning options.
* **Queue & Donation Management:** Game queues, pre-queues, donation review panels, and queue position tracking (`/myqueue`).
* **Automated Keyword Triggers:** Custom auto-responses based on string matching (`contains`, `exact`, `startswith`).
